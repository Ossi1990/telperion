Core Modules (BASIC Tier)

Office Workstation
Requirements: Standard office PC with MS Excel and a web browser. No CAD license required!

1. SSoT Order Aggregator (Batch Consolidator)

Merges unit SSoT files (each for 1 pc of a product) into a Consolidated Order Parts List. Multiplies quantities by batch sizes and sums shared components across assemblies.

2. Production & Purchasing Report Generator

From a unit SSoT or Consolidated Order Parts List creates: Material & Purchasing Summary (steel kg, profiles m, hardware pcs, wood & glass m², subcontracting alerts), Paint Estimator [L/m²], and PDF Workstation Cards (Saw, Laser, Press Brake, Lathes/Milling, Subcontracting, Wood, Glass, Welding, plus Loading & Assembly Summary with conservation notes).

Computes optimal cutting layouts for 6 m and 12 m stock bars including saw kerf width. Features routing filters (1D wood, subcontracting), manual item exclusion, and graphical cutting bars for saw operators.

PRO Modules (CAD Engineering)

SolidWorks Workstation
Requirements: Engineering workstation with SolidWorks + MS Excel. (Currently available exclusively for SolidWorks).

4. 3D Metadata & Machine File Harvester

Assists sheet-metal conversion (Insert Bends) in Human-in-the-Loop mode under technologist control, exports 1:1 DXF and STEP files, calculates bounding boxes (A×B, L), mass, paint area, and auto-detects wood and glass.

5. CAD SSoT Structure Generator (1 Unit of Product)

Builds a multi-level, multi-column master SSoT structure (for 1 unit of a single product: welding & final assembly) directly from the SolidWorks tree, with default workstation routing.

6. SSoT Data Integrator (2D & 3D Bridge)

Links existing part lists (e.g., from 2D drawings) with 3D CAD metadata into a complete product SSoT. Matches components by normalized part names and populates dimensions, thicknesses, and weights.
